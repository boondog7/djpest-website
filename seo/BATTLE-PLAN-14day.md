7. **Fix Google Fonts render-block** → Performance 73→90+. Quick known win.
8. **Hub-and-spoke internal linking** + **AI/LLM optimisation** (structured Q&A, price tables = get cited by ChatGPT/Perplexity/AI Overviews).
