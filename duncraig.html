What's your eave treatment for spiders and wasps?

We treat eaves, gutters, and roof junctions with a low-toxicity spray that prevents nesting but doesn't harm the tree ecosystem. Duncraig's tree coverage makes this quarterly. We can schedule around your season preferences (fewer treatments in spring bird-nesting season).

Is remedial termite damage expensive?

It depends on extent. If we find active termites, we treat immediately (costs included in your quote). If we find damage but termites are gone, structural repairs are separate — we recommend a builder's report. Our termite treatment protects against future damage regardless of past history.

How often should I get inspections in Duncraig?
