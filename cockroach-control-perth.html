Cockroach species in Perth aren't all the same.

Three species cause 95% of Perth cockroach calls — and they each need different chemistry, placement, and follow-up. German cockroach (Blattella germanica) is the small kitchen one — 12-15mm, tan with two dark stripes, lives in warm humid voids near food and water. They breed insanely fast (one female = 30-40 offspring per egg case, 4-8 cases per lifetime). American cockroach (Periplaneta americana) is the big reddish one (35-40mm) that flies into your kitchen from the drain at 11pm. They're sewer/sub-floor specialists. Australian cockroach (Periplaneta australasiae) looks similar to American but has yellow wing markings — outdoor, garden/wood pile breeders.

The reason most "cockroach treatments" fail is they spray-and-go without ID, without IGR, and without addressing the void/drain breeding sites. Spray kills the 5% that are out walking around. The other 95% are inside walls, behind appliances, and inside hatching egg cases — and they breed back faster than the survivors die.

Why Perth cockroach pressure is high

  • Warm climate — Perth's mild winters mean cockroaches breed year-round. No seasonal die-off like cooler cities.
  • Multi-unit dwellings — apartments and townhouses share wall cavities and plumbing, so Germans travel between units.
  • Restaurants & cafes — Perth's food density means continuous re-invasion pressure on commercial kitchens.
  • Older inner-suburb plumbing — clay or cracked sewer connections are American cockroach highways.
  • Bushland-adjacent properties — Australian cockroach pressure from outdoor sources (wood piles, mulch beds).

How we treat cockroaches (properly).

1. ID the species — different chemistry, different placements

German cockroach jobs are gel + IGR inside cabinetry. American cockroach jobs are drain treatment + perimeter. Australian cockroach jobs are external harbourage clean-up + perimeter spray. Generic "cockroach spray" treats none of them properly.

2. Cabinetry gel + IGR (Insect Growth Regulator)

Modern cockroach treatment isn't about killing adults — it's about breaking the reproduction cycle. IGR (typically pyriproxyfen or hydroprene) prevents juveniles from maturing into reproductive adults. Even if some current adults survive, they can't be replaced. Gel placements (hydrazide active) go inside cabinetry voids, behind appliances, and on hinge sides of doors. Food-safe placement only — never on food-contact surfaces.

3. Drain treatment for American/Australian species

For drain-breeding species we treat the floor wastes, sub-floor cavities, and external drain pits with a residual product safe for plumbing. Sealing service penetrations (where pipes enter the slab) prevents re-invasion.

4. Targeted perimeter — no kitchen interior spray

External perimeter spray (around door frames, vents, weep holes, garage entries) creates a barrier. We do not spray kitchen or bathroom interiors with residual product — gel + IGR is more effective and avoids food-prep surface contamination.

Frequently asked about cockroaches.

What kind of cockroach do I have?

German cockroaches are small (12-15mm), tan/light brown with two dark stripes, and live indoors in kitchens, bathrooms, and warm appliances. American cockroaches are large (35-40mm), reddish-brown, fast-moving, and prefer drains, sewers, and outdoor sub-floors. Australian cockroaches are similar size to American but have yellow markings on their wings and prefer outdoor wood piles and gardens. We ID before we treat — the chemistry differs.

How much does cockroach treatment cost in Perth?

From $250 for a standard cockroach treatment — IGR (insect growth regulator), cabinetry gel placements, and targeted perimeter where appropriate. German cockroach jobs in apartments or shared walls run higher (from $320) because we need to coordinate with neighbouring units. Commercial kitchens are quoted on assessment.

Are cockroaches dangerous?

They don't bite, but they're a Category 2 health risk in food premises. They mechanically transfer Salmonella, E. coli, and triggers for asthma. They walk through drains and refuse, then walk across food preparation surfaces. The food safety implications are why commercial kitchens have continuous cockroach programs — it's a regulatory requirement.

Why are cockroaches so hard to get rid of?

German cockroaches reproduce extremely quickly — one female produces 30-40 offspring per egg case, and females carry 4-8 cases in a lifetime. A starting population of 10 can become 1,000+ in 8 weeks. They also develop chemical resistance fast — sites that have been hit with the same active ingredient repeatedly often have populations that no longer respond to it. We rotate actives and use IGR (insect growth regulator) which prevents juveniles from maturing — breaks the reproduction cycle even if some adults survive.

Are roach baits safe around food prep?

Yes. We use food-safe gel baits placed inside cabinetry voids, behind appliances, and on hinge sides of doors — never on food-contact surfaces. We avoid spraying inside kitchen interiors entirely. Active ingredients are at very low concentrations, food-safe per APVMA labelling. We brief you on placements and re-entry times.

How long until they're gone?

German cockroach populations typically clear within 14-21 days. American/Australian cockroach (drain-based) jobs clear faster — 7-10 days — because we can directly treat the breeding sites. Heavily-infested or apartment-block jobs may need a 4-week follow-up program. IGR keeps working for 90+ days post-treatment.

Why does spray-on-its-own not work?

Cockroaches that are awake and moving when you spray die. Cockroaches inside wall voids, behind appliances, and inside hatching egg cases are unaffected. So you kill 5% and the other 95% breed back faster than the survivors die. Modern treatment is gel + IGR + targeted spray for surfaces, with the IGR doing the heavy lifting by stopping juveniles maturing. That's why it actually works.

Do you treat cockroaches in apartments?

Yes, but apartment treatment is more complex. German cockroaches travel between units through wall cavities and shared plumbing. If only your unit gets treated and the neighbours don't, re-infestation is highly likely. We'll talk through the realistic options — body corporate-coordinated treatment is ideal, but failing that we can treat your unit aggressively and seal entry points to slow re-invasion.
