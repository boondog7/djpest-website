Commonly asked.

How long does treatment take?
Most treatments take 1–2 hours. Termite inspections and treatments may take longer depending on property size and pest type. We'll give you an exact estimate when you call.
What's your warranty?
3 months on general pest and rodent services. 8 years on termite treatments when completed to AS 3660.2 standards. Warranty is documented and transferable. If treatment fails within warranty period, we return at no charge.
Are you licensed?
We're working toward full licensure under CPP30119. Provisional licence pending. Dane operates under Danny Johns' established license during transition. Full Endorsement 2 (Commercial & Domestic) targeted late 2026.
Do I need to leave the house?
For most treatments, you don't need to leave. Some spray treatments require temporary absence of pets. We'll brief you before starting. Termite treatments may require longer ventilation periods — we'll advise.
How quickly can you respond?
Most quotes returned within 2 hours. Most bookings available within 48 hours. Active termite infestations get same-day or next-day service. Call 0447 747 769 for urgent bookings.
What areas do you cover?
All of Perth metro — from Joondalup in the north to Kwinana in the south, east to Kalamunda, west to the coast. Single service area, no zone restrictions.
How much does treatment cost?
Pricing depends on property size, pest type, and treatment method. Quotes are free. Call 0447 747 769 or submit the form below and we'll send a detailed quote within 2 hours.
Will you inspect before treating?
Always. We identify the pest species, assess entry points, check for harborage, and rule out conditions that might affect treatment. Most treatments include a walk-through with recommendations.
