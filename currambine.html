Why Currambine gets the pests it gets

The suburb name was approved in 1980 but the houses came in the mid-1990s, after Currambine station opened on the Joondalup line in 1993. Almost everything here is slab-on-ground brick and tile, or two-storey with steel and timber framing, laid out in quarters by Connolly Drive and Moore Drive. Slab construction has no timber in the ground, but termites still enter through slab penetrations, cracked edges and the garden beds and paving built up against the brickwork, and many 1990s homes have had their original management zone bridged by landscaping. The ground is yellow Spearwood sand over Tamala limestone, the heaps of rocks in the name, and limestone close to the surface means most chemical systems here go in by drilling and injecting.

Five holes of the Joondalup Golf Course sit in the south-eastern corner of Currambine, and its irrigated fairways, lakes and bush edges keep rodents, mosquitoes and coastal brown ants well supplied on the blocks that back onto them. Delamere Park and the Currambine Community Centre behind Currambine Central are the green heart of the suburb, with mature trees and retained vegetation that carry black house spiders and native cockroaches onto nearby homes. Burns Beach Road and the freeway verges add rat harbourage along the northern and eastern edges. We tailor treatments to the golf course blocks and to the tight-packed streets near the station where colonies run across several fences.

Mosquitoes

Questions

Asked in Currambine.

How long does it take you to reach Currambine?

About twenty minutes from Warwick up the Mitchell Freeway to Shenton Avenue or Burns Beach Road. Same-day for active pests is usually possible, and routine treatments are booked within the week.

My Currambine home is on a slab. Do I still need a termite inspection?

Yes. Slab-on-ground removes timber from the ground but not the risk: termites come through plumbing and electrical penetrations, shrinkage cracks and any point where paving or garden beds have been built above the slab edge. Many 1990s homes have had their original management zone bridged that way. An inspection to AS 4349.3 every twelve months is the standard. Book an inspection.

Why are the ants in my Currambine driveway so persistent?

Because coastal brown ants here live in super-colonies with many queens, linked across neighbouring blocks under paving and retaining walls. A repellent spray only splits the colony and moves it. We treat with a slow-acting non-repellent product and bait to label so the workers carry it back and the colony collapses. Read more.

Do the golf course lakes cause mosquito problems in Currambine?

On the blocks that back onto the Joondalup golf course, yes, especially on still spring and summer evenings. We treat the shaded vegetation where adult mosquitoes rest, applied to label, and walk your block for the pot saucers, gutters and retic pits that breed them close to the house. Mosquito management.
