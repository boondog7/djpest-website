Why most ant treatments fail in Perth.

Perth's biggest ant problem is the coastal brown ant (Pheidole megacephala), and conventional pest control makes it dramatically worse. Here's why: coastal brown ants form super-colonies — networks of linked nests that share queens, workers, and resources across hundreds of metres. When you hit them with a repellent spray, the colony detects the threat and "buds" — splits into multiple satellite nests to escape. What was one nest under the patio becomes seven nests across the property in weeks.

The fix is non-repellent chemistry — fipronil-based or hydrazide gel baits that workers don't detect. They walk through them, carry the active ingredient back to the colony in their crops, and feed it to nest-mates and queens. The whole colony collapses, not just the foragers you see on the kitchen counter. Bunnings ant killer doesn't do this. It can't.

How we treat ants (species first).

1. ID the species before we touch chemistry

Coastal brown vs Argentine vs white-footed vs sugar — they each respond differently. Coastal brown super-colonies need fipronil non-repellent. White-footed house ants respond best to hydrazide gel. Sugar ants accept sweet liquid baits. Generic ant spray treats none of them properly.

2. Map the trails (not the nests)

For super-colony species there's no "the nest" — there are dozens, linked. We map the active foraging trails, identify entry points to the structure, and place bait stations along those paths. The trail map is the treatment plan.

3. Non-repellent transfer baiting

External: fipronil-based granular bait or liquid bait stations, placed along trails and near suspected nest entries. Internal: food-safe hydrazide gel placement under appliances, behind cabinetry, and at known entry points (around dishwasher hose entries, sink waste pipes, sliding door tracks). No spraying of food-prep surfaces.

4. Expect activity to spike before it drops

For 3-7 days after treatment you'll see more ants — they're recruiting nest-mates to the bait, which is exactly what we want. Don't spray in panic. Activity then drops sharply by week 2. Full super-colony collapse takes 4-6 weeks. Smaller localised colonies clear in 7-14 days.

Frequently asked about ants.

Why has spraying made my ant problem worse?

Because you're probably dealing with a coastal brown ant super-colony. Repellent sprays (Bunnings ant killers, perimeter sprays based on bifenthrin) trigger "budding" — the colony splits into multiple satellite nests to escape the threat. What was one nest under your patio becomes seven nests across the property within weeks. The fix is non-repellent chemistry — fipronil-based or hydrazide gel baits — that workers don't detect, walk through, and carry back to collapse the whole colony.

How long does ant baiting take to work?

You'll see a noticeable spike in ant activity for 3-7 days as they recruit nest-mates to the bait. That's the bait working — they're carrying it back. Activity then drops sharply by week 2. Full colony collapse for super-colonies typically takes 4-6 weeks. Smaller localised colonies clear in 7-14 days.

Are ant baits safe for pets and kids?

Yes when applied properly. Bait stations are tamper-resistant and placed where pets and kids can't reach (under appliances, behind cabinetry, sealed exterior locations). Active ingredients are at very low concentrations — toxic to ants, well below mammalian effect thresholds at the dose used. We brief you on placements and re-entry times.

What's a coastal brown ant super-colony?

Coastal brown ants (Pheidole megacephala) form linked nest networks that share genetics and resources across hundreds of metres. Multiple queens, multiple nest sites, no inter-nest aggression. In Perth they thrive in sandy soil, paving cracks, garden bed edges, and lawn margins — particularly in beachside suburbs (Hillarys, Sorrento, Mullaloo, Edgewater). You can't "find the nest" because there isn't one — there are dozens.

Can you treat ants inside the kitchen?

Yes. Internal treatment is gel-based (hydrazide active, food-safe placement behind cabinetry and under appliances). We don't spray kitchen interiors with residual product — the gel approach is more effective and avoids food-prep surface contamination. Standard 3-month warranty on internal work.

Do you guarantee complete elimination?

We guarantee that you'll see the colony collapse within the treatment window, and our 3-month warranty covers re-treatment if active ant trails reappear. We don't claim "100% gone forever" — coastal brown ants in particular have re-invasion pressure from neighbouring properties, and an annual top-up is realistic for chronic super-colony pressure zones.
