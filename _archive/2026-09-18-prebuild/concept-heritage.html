04Our story

A trade handed down.

In 1998, Danny Johns began treating homes across Perth's northern suburbs under the WDJ Pest Control name. Twenty-five years on, the ladder still goes up the same way, the inspection still starts in the same corners, and more than a thousand families know the van when it pulls into the drive.

DJ Pest is the next chapter — the same trade, the same standards, now run by his son Dane Johns: a Chartered Accountant who chose the family craft and trained for it formally under CPP30119. The result is a business that treats your home like a tradesman and keeps its records like an accountant.

Through the provisional licence period, Dane works under WDJ Pest Control's licensed pest-management supervisor — the standard supervised pathway in Western Australia — carrying $20M in public liability cover. The hands are second-generation. The paperwork has never been tighter.

06Good questions

The honest answers.

The things people ask before they book — including the licensing and warranty questions we think you should ask.

Q1Is DJ Pest fully licensed?
Dane Johns is currently working on a provisional licence under WDJ Pest Control's licensed pest-management supervisor — the standard supervised pathway for new technicians in Western Australia. Every job carries $20M public liability cover, and full endorsement is targeted for late 2026. We'd rather state this plainly than overclaim.
Q2How do the warranties actually work?
Each treatment is closed with a written, dated warranty: 8 years on termite work to AS 3660.2, 90 days on spider perimeter treatments, and 3 months on general, ant and rodent work. If the covered pests return within that period, we come back to re-treat. The warranty is documented and transferable to the next owner, and it operates alongside your rights under the Australian Consumer Law — never instead of them.
Q3How quickly can you get to me?
For active termites we aim for a same-day response — that's the one you don't sit on. Written quotes are typically turned around within about two hours, and bookings usually land within 48 hours. We're contactable 7am–5pm, seven days.
Q4Which suburbs do you cover?
Our roots are in Perth's northern suburbs — Joondalup, Hillarys, Duncraig, Kingsley, Warwick, Wanneroo, Currambine, Mindarie, Sorrento, Greenwood and Padbury — and the surrounding coast. If you're nearby and not sure, call and ask.
Q5What products do you use around children and pets?
We use APVMA-registered products applied strictly to label, and we choose the method to suit the household — for example tamper-resistant stations for rodents and targeted baiting for ants. We'll always walk you through what's being applied and any label directions to follow before we begin.
Q6Why a redback spider as the brand?
Because the redback knows every corner, weep hole and eave of a Perth home better than anyone. We took that as our guardian — the one keeping watch over the house, not the one you're afraid of. It's a quietly Australian way of saying we're paying attention to the places pests actually hide.
