Top priority

Termites

Highest priority

Silent timber damage that often shows no surface signs until it's serious. Perth's sandy soils and warm climate make subterranean termites a year-round risk for most homes.

Read the guide

Redback Spider

Medically significant

A Perth backyard regular found in sheds, letterboxes, pot bases and under outdoor furniture. Bites can be medically significant — recognising the spider and its messy web matters.

Read the guide

Rats & Mice

Property & health

Roof rats and house mice gnaw wiring, contaminate food and breed fast in roof voids and wall cavities. The first signs are usually droppings, scratching at night and a musty smell.

Read the guide

Cockroaches

Hygiene risk

German and Australian cockroaches thrive in warm kitchens, sub-floors and drains. They spread bacteria across food surfaces and can trigger allergies — and a few seen usually means many unseen.
