003 / FAQ

Straight
answers.

The questions Perth homeowners actually ask — answered plainly.

Q1Is DJ Pest fully licensed?
Dane Johns is currently on a provisional pest-management licence and works under the supervision of WDJ Pest Control’s licensed pest-management supervisor — the standard supervised pathway in Western Australia. Full endorsement is targeted for late 2026. Every job is carried out within that supervised framework and is fully insured.
Q2How do your warranties work?
Termite treatments to AS 3660.2 carry an 8-year warranty; general pest and rodent treatments carry 3 months; spider treatments carry 90 days. Each is documented in writing and transferable with the property. If covered pests return within the period, we come back at no extra cost. These warranties operate alongside your Australian Consumer Law rights, not instead of them.
Q3How fast can you get to me?
For active termites we aim for a same-day response, because that’s the one pest where days matter. Quotes are typically returned within about two hours, and standard bookings are usually within 48 hours. We operate 7am–5pm, seven days.
Q4Which Perth suburbs do you cover?
Our roots are in Perth’s northern suburbs — Joondalup, Hillarys, Duncraig, Kingsley, Warwick, Wanneroo, Currambine, Mindarie, Sorrento, Greenwood and Padbury — with more than a thousand homes treated across the area. If you’re nearby and unsure, just call and ask.
Q5What products do you use around my family?
We use APVMA-registered products applied strictly to label, choosing the method that suits the pest and the property — transfer baits for ant colonies, non-repellent perimeters for termites, sealed tamper-resistant stations for rodents. We’ll always talk you through what we’re applying and where before we start.
Q6Why a redback spider as your brand?
The redback is one of the pests we manage — and the most recognisable in Perth. We use it as a mark of guard rather than threat: the watchful presence on the wall is us, keeping your home protected. It’s a family business with a long memory, and the mark reflects that.
