[05] — FAQ
How fast can you get to me?
Most quotes come back the same day, and most bookings land inside 48 hours. Active termites or a rodent emergency get same-day or next-day priority — call 0447 747 769 for anything urgent.
What's actually covered by the warranty?
8 years on termite treatments completed to AS 3660.2, 3 months on general pest and rodent work, 90 days on spider perimeters. It's documented in writing and transferable to a new owner. If covered pests return inside the period, we come back at no charge. This sits alongside your rights under Australian Consumer Law — it doesn't replace them.
Are you licensed and insured?
Yes. We carry $20M public liability insurance, and Dane operates under WDJ Pest Control's licensed pest-management technician during his provisional period under CPP30119 — the standard supervised pathway for every WA technician. Full endorsement is targeted for late 2026.
Do I need to leave the house?
Usually not. Some treatments ask for pets to be out of the area for a short window, and termite work may need extra ventilation time. We brief you before we start and only ever apply APVMA-registered products to label.
What suburbs do you cover?
All of Perth metro, with our roots in the northern suburbs — Joondalup, Hillarys, Duncraig, Kingsley, Warwick, Wanneroo and out from there. One service area, no zone surcharges.
How much does it cost?
It depends on the property, the pest and the method, so quotes are free and specific — no lowball-then-upsell. Run the risk check above or call and we'll have a real number to you, usually within a couple of hours.
