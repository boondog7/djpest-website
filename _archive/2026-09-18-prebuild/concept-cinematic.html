Good questions

Answered straight.

Is DJ Pest fully licensed?
Dane Johns is completing his pest-technician qualification (CPP30119) and is currently on a provisional licence. During this period he works under WDJ Pest Control's licensed pest-management supervisor — the standard supervised pathway in Western Australia. Full endorsement is targeted for late 2026. We're upfront about exactly where we sit.
How do your warranties work — and what about my consumer rights?
Termite treatments carry an 8-year warranty to AS 3660.2; general pest and rodent work carry 3 months; spider treatments carry 90 days. Every warranty is documented in writing and transferable to a new owner. If a covered pest returns within its period, we come back. These warranties operate alongside — not instead of — your rights under the Australian Consumer Law.
How fast can you respond?
Active termites get a same-day response wherever possible — they're a structural threat and we treat them that way. For most other enquiries we aim to have a quote to you within about two hours, and a booking usually within 48 hours. We're available 7am–5pm, seven days.
What about my family and pets when you treat?
We only use APVMA-registered products, applied strictly to label — the label is the law, and it sets out where and how each product can be used. We'll talk you through any preparation before we treat, such as keeping pets and children off treated areas until surfaces are dry, and we tailor the method to your home and situation.
Which areas do you cover?
We're rooted in Perth's northern suburbs — Joondalup, Hillarys, Duncraig, Kingsley, Warwick, Wanneroo, Currambine, Mindarie, Sorrento, Greenwood and Padbury — and the surrounding corridor. If you're nearby and not sure, give us a call and we'll tell you straight away.
Do I really need a timber-pest inspection before buying?
For most Perth buyers it's well worth it. Our inspections are carried out to AS 4349.3 and come with a written report and photographs, so you head into a purchase knowing the real condition of the timber rather than guessing. It's one of the cheapest forms of certainty you can buy on a home.
