Why Clarkson gets the pests it gets

Clarkson was pastoral land until the early 1990s, then built out fast: the first stages around Marmion Avenue and Connolly Drive, the north-east around the community high school from 1995, and the Somerly estate around Clarkson station through the 2000s. Nearly every home is a slab-on-ground brick and tile or brick and Colorbond build with steel or treated timber roof framing, plus two-storey apartments along Ocean Keys Boulevard and Ningaloo Bend. The ground is Quindalup coastal dune sand on the western side, about two kilometres from the ocean, becoming yellow sand over Tamala limestone caprock toward the railway line and Neerabup Road. Newer homes carry less timber, but termites still enter through slab penetrations, unsealed expansion joints and landscaping piled against the slab edge.

The Yanchep line separates Clarkson from Neerabup National Park, a long strip of banksia woodland and limestone caprock that runs the length of the eastern boundary. That bush is home to native cockroaches, huntsmen, redbacks and a large rodent population that moves into the estates along the railway side in winter. Anthony Waring Park, Richard Aldersea Park and the many smaller Somerly parks link the bush to the houses with turf, mulch and fence lines. On new estates, the treatment is about the slab, the boundary and the neighbour: we check every penetration and the treated zone under the paving, we proof roof and eave gaps that builders leave, and we keep the internal treatment low-odour and non-staining.

What we treat most in Clarkson

The Clarkson pest short-list.

Termites

Slab penetrations, bridged treated zones under paving and alfresco areas, and neighbour pressure from the Neerabup bush. Inspections to AS 4349.3, and management systems to AS 3660.2 installed to work with the slab, not against it. Termite inspection.

Coastal brown ants

Clarkson is coastal brown ant heartland: warm white sand under paving, retaining walls and pool surrounds across every estate. Colony-level treatment with a non-repellent product or bait, APVMA-registered and applied to its label. Ant management.

Rats and mice

Neerabup National Park and the railway reserve feed rats into the eastern streets; mice move through the apartments and townhouses near Ocean Keys. Proofing the gaps builders leave at eaves and garages, then secured bait stations. Rodent management.

Spiders and cockroaches

Redbacks under limestone coping and in retic boxes, huntsmen and black house spiders off the bush, American cockroaches from the newer stormwater and sewer network. General pest treatment.

What's active now

This season in Clarkson.

Spring in Clarkson means coastal brown ants first, everywhere there is paving, followed by redbacks reappearing along limestone walls and under outdoor furniture. Homes on the railway side start seeing spiders and native cockroaches come in from the Neerabup bush as it dries out. Even in a suburb this young, the first-generation 1990s homes are now thirty years old, so a termite inspection before the November swarm is worth booking.

Questions

Asked in Clarkson.

How long does it take you to reach Clarkson?

About twenty-five minutes from Warwick up the Mitchell Freeway and Neerabup Road, or Marmion Avenue. Same-day for active pests is usually possible, and Clarkson is on the same run as Mindarie, Butler and Kinross.

My Clarkson home is newer. Do I still need a termite inspection?

Yes. The slab is not a barrier on its own. Termites enter through plumbing penetrations, expansion joints and any spot where paving, garden beds or an alfresco slab bridge the treated zone, and the bush across the railway line keeps the pressure up. An inspection to AS 4349.3 every twelve months, and a management system to AS 3660.2 where activity is found. Book an inspection.

Why are coastal brown ants so bad in Clarkson?

Warm, well-drained coastal sand under paving is the habitat they were named for, and every estate in Clarkson is built on it. They form linked super-colonies with many queens across several lots. Repellent sprays fragment the colony; a slow-acting non-repellent product or bait, applied to label, collapses it. Read more.

I back onto the railway and the Neerabup bush. What should I expect?

Rodents in winter and spiders in spring. The bush and railway reserve are year-round habitat, and builders leave gaps at eaves, garage doors and roof penetrations that rats find quickly. We proof those gaps, set secured bait stations along the boundary, and treat the bush-facing eaves and fence lines for spiders. Rodent management.

What does a general pest treatment in Clarkson involve?

An internal and external treatment for spiders, cockroaches and ants, low-odour and non-staining indoors, with a roof-void check where access allows. A typical three-bedroom home, internal and external, sits in the $250 to $350 range. Every job is quoted itemised in writing. See the investment guide.
